# WhatsApp Automation for Exporters: Never Miss an Overseas Buyer

If you export, your buyers are in different time zones. An importer in Dubai or Frankfurt sends a WhatsApp enquiry while you're asleep — and by morning they've asked three other suppliers. Speed wins export deals.

We run an export business ourselves (Darjeeling tea and agricultural produce), so we built the same automation we now set up for other businesses.

## What WhatsApp automation handles

- **Instant replies, 24/7** — greet every enquiry, ask the right qualifying questions (product, quantity, destination port).
- **Lead capture** — every enquiry saved with the buyer's details so nothing gets lost in a chat thread.
- **Catalog sharing** — send product sheets, specs and certifications on request.
- **Order updates** — automatic messages when an order is packed, shipped, or documents are ready.

## Add AI voice for calls

Some buyers still prefer to call. AI voice agents can:
- Answer inbound calls and triage enquiries when your team is busy or offline.
- Book follow-up calls or video meetings into your calendar.
- Make outbound calls to follow up on quotes and samples.

## What it looks like in practice

1. A buyer messages: "Need 2 tons of garlic, CIF Jebel Ali."
2. The bot replies instantly, asks for grade and packing, and shares the spec sheet.
3. Their details land in your lead list; your team gets a summary.
4. If they call instead, the voice agent captures the same details and books a callback.

## Is it right for you?

It's a good fit if you:
- Get enquiries outside office hours or from multiple countries.
- Answer the same questions (MOQ, price basis, lead time) again and again.
- Lose track of leads across personal phones and chats.
