## The problem: WhatsApp is busy, and replies are slow

WhatsApp is where many customers start a conversation. They ask about price, stock, delivery or availability, and they expect a quick answer.

Doing that by hand doesn't scale. The same questions get typed out over and over. Messages arrive after hours and sit unread until morning. Leads live on someone's personal phone, so when that person is away, the enquiry is lost. And customers who have already ordered keep messaging to ask, "Where is my order?"
