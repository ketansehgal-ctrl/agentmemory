## The problem every case study starts with

Most of the businesses we work with have the same core problem: customers reach out by website, phone and WhatsApp, and the business can't respond fast enough on every channel. Each case study below shows where enquiries were getting lost and what we built to fix it.

## How we approach each project

1. **Find the gap.** Where do enquiries, orders or customers get stuck today?
2. **Build the fix.** A website, app, SaaS product, voice automation, WhatsApp automation, or a combination.
3. **Connect it.** Make sure every channel works together instead of as separate tools.
4. **Measure honestly.** Compare before and after using real data from the business.
