## The problem: too many vendors, too many missed enquiries

Most growing businesses end up stitching together a web agency, an app developer, a chatbot provider and a call-answering service. Each one has its own contract, its own login and its own idea of how things should work. When something breaks between them, nobody owns it.

Meanwhile, customers keep calling and messaging. Calls go unanswered after hours, WhatsApp chats sit unread, and leads disappear into personal phones. The tools are there, but they don't work together.
