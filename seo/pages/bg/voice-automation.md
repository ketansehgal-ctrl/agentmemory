## The problem: calls you can't answer are customers you lose

A customer who calls wants an answer now, not a voicemail. But your team can only take so many calls at once, and nobody is at the desk after hours.

Outbound calling has the opposite problem. New leads go cold while your team works through a list by hand, and the same follow-up questions get asked again and again.

The result is the same either way: missed calls, slow follow-ups and enquiries that never reach the right person.

## Outbound calls

- **Lead outreach.** Call new leads soon after they enquire, ask qualifying questions and pass interested prospects to your sales team.
- **Sales follow-ups.** Follow up on quotes, samples or demos without your team dialling each number.
- **Reminders and confirmations.** Call customers to confirm appointments or upcoming visits.

## Inbound calls and enquiry handling

- **Customer support.** Answer common questions about your products, services, location and process.
- **Appointment booking.** Let callers pick a time and book it during the call.
- **Enquiry triage.** Find out what the caller needs, capture their details and route the enquiry to the right person or team.
- **After-hours cover.** Calls outside office hours still get a real conversation instead of a missed-call notification.
