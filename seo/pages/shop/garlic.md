# Garlic Exporter from India

Indian garlic is known for a strong, pungent flavour that holds up in cooking, pickling and processing. Quantum Accord Business Group exports garlic in bulk to importers, wholesalers and food manufacturers, and packs each order to the bulb size and format your customers expect.

## How garlic buyers specify an order

Garlic is bought on a handful of measurable points. Before quoting, we will ask you about:

- **Bulb diameter.** Usually expressed in millimetres. Larger bulbs typically suit retail; smaller sizes often go to processing.
- **Clove structure.** Indian garlic types differ in clove size and count per bulb. Some markets prefer fewer, larger cloves; processors may prefer smaller cloves.
- **Skin colour.** From clean white to purple-streaked, depending on the type.
- **Dryness.** Well-dried garlic with papery outer skin stores much longer and is less prone to mould.
- **Trim.** How the stem and roots are cut.

## Storage and transit

Garlic is a dry-stored crop. Good airflow and low humidity keep it firm and stop sprouting and mould on long voyages, which is why breathable packing and the right container setting matter as much as the garlic itself.
