## Size and quality

<!-- image: onion-size-grading.jpg alt="Onions sorted by diameter into size grades on a packing table" -->

Onions are usually graded by bulb diameter in millimetres, and buyers often specify a band such as small, medium or large for each order. Beyond size, an importer will typically check:

- **Neck and skin.** A tight, dry neck and intact outer skin help the bulb resist rot in transit.
- **Firmness.** No soft, sprouted or hollow bulbs.
- **Defects.** Limits on cuts, bruising, black mould and doubles.
- **Curing.** Properly cured onions store and travel far better than freshly lifted ones.
