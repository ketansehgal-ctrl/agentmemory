# About Quantum Accord Business Group

**Connecting India to the World.**

Quantum Accord Business Group is an export company based in Darjeeling, India. We help importers, distributors and international sourcing partners buy Indian products with confidence — starting with the tea our home region is famous for.

## Rooted in Darjeeling

Darjeeling sits high in the hills of West Bengal, and its name is known worldwide because of one product: tea. Darjeeling tea is protected by a Geographical Indication, which means only tea from this region can carry the name. Being based here keeps us close to the product that leads our range.
