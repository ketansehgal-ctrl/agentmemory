# Makhana (Foxnut) Exporter from India

Makhana — also sold as foxnuts or fox nuts, and often marketed as "puffed lotus seeds" — is the popped seed of the *Euryale ferox* plant, an aquatic crop grown in ponds and wetlands. India is the world's main producer of makhana, and it has moved quickly from a traditional ingredient to a mainstream snack in health-food aisles abroad.

Quantum Accord Business Group exports makhana in bulk to importers, distributors, snack manufacturers and brands that want a private-label product.

## Why buyers are adding makhana

- **A light, crunchy snack base.** Popped makhana takes seasoning well, which makes it a natural fit for roasted and flavoured snack lines.
- **Plant-based and gluten-free by nature.** It appeals to shoppers looking for alternatives to fried snacks. (Check claims against your market's labelling rules.)
- **Versatile in the kitchen.** Used in curries, desserts and kheer in Indian cooking, and as an ingredient in trail mixes and cereal blends.

## Grading: size matters

<!-- image: makhana-size-grades.jpg alt="Three piles of makhana sorted by size from small to large" -->

Makhana is traded largely on size and appearance. In the Indian trade, size is usually expressed in *suta* (sometimes spelled "sutta") — a larger suta number means bigger, rounder seeds that command a higher price. Buyers also look at colour (whiter is generally preferred), the share of broken or unpopped pieces, and moisture, which affects crunch and shelf life.

When you enquire, tell us the size grade, the acceptable level of broken pieces and how the product will be used. Snack brands roasting whole pieces usually need larger grades, while ingredient buyers may accept smaller or mixed sizes at a lower price.
