# Potato Exporter from India

The best potato for one buyer is the wrong potato for another. A supermarket wants clean, even tubers that look good on the shelf; a chip factory cares about dry matter and sugar levels that control frying colour. Quantum Accord Business Group exports potatoes in bulk and starts every enquiry by asking what the potatoes are for.

## Table potatoes vs processing potatoes

- **Table (fresh-market) potatoes** go to retail, wholesale markets and food service. Appearance comes first: uniform size, smooth skin, shallow eyes and no greening.
- **Processing potatoes** are bought for chips, crisps, fries and flakes. Processors usually specify dry matter (or specific gravity) and low reducing sugars, because these determine texture and how golden the fried product turns out.

## Grading and inspection

<!-- image: potato-grading-line.jpg alt="Potatoes moving along a grading line being sorted by size" -->

Potatoes are commonly sorted by tuber size or weight into bands. Typical quality points a buyer will want checked include:

- Skin colour (white, yellow or red) and finish
- Freedom from greening, cracks, cuts and bruising
- No sprouting, soft rot or pest damage
- Soil level — brushed or washed as agreed

## Storage and shipping

Potatoes are living tubers. They must be kept cool, dark and well ventilated to stop sprouting and greening, and handled gently to avoid bruising that shows up days later.
