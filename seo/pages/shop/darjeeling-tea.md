# Darjeeling Tea Exporter from India

Darjeeling tea is our flagship product. Quantum Accord Business Group is based in Darjeeling, and we supply importers, distributors and private-label brands with tea from the hills that gave it its name — in bulk for blending and repacking, or packed under your own label.

## Why Darjeeling tea is different

Darjeeling tea is protected by a **Geographical Indication (GI)**. Only tea grown and processed in designated gardens of the Darjeeling hills in West Bengal can legally be sold as "Darjeeling." High elevation, cool temperatures and slow leaf growth give it the light, aromatic cup that sets it apart from other black teas — and lets importers sell it at a premium.

## Flushes: four seasons, four characters

| Flush | Approx. season | Cup character | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|

| Second flush | May–June | Fuller body, muscatel note | Signature Darjeeling retail packs |
| Monsoon | July–September | Stronger, darker | Blends and value ranges |
| Autumnal | October–November | Mellow, rounded, coppery | Everyday and gift ranges |

Read more: [First flush vs second flush Darjeeling](/blog/first-flush-vs-second-flush-darjeeling).

## Grades

Grades describe leaf size and appearance, not origin. Whole-leaf grades such as **SFTGFOP1, FTGFOP1 and TGFOP** carry more tips and suit premium loose tea. Broken grades (**BOP**) brew faster and stronger, while **fannings and dust** are used mainly in tea bags. Full guide: [Darjeeling tea grades explained](/blog/darjeeling-tea-grades-explained).
