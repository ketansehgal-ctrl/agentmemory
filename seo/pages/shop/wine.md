## What to have ready before you enquire

Wine moves under tighter rules than food products. To speed up a quote, have these to hand:

- **Your import licence or permit** details for the destination country.
- **Label requirements** for your market — alcohol by volume, allergen statements (such as sulphites), importer details, health warnings and language.
- **Volumes and mix** — which wines, how many cases of each, and whether you need a mixed container.
- **Duty and excise arrangements** — usually handled by the importer at destination.
