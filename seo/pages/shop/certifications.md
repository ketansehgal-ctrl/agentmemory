## What each one means for you

### FSSAI

The Food Safety and Standards Authority of India regulates food businesses in India. An FSSAI licence shows we are registered to handle and trade food products under Indian food safety law.

### FIEO

The Federation of Indian Export Organisations is the umbrella body for export promotion in India, set up with the support of the Ministry of Commerce. Membership connects us to India's official export ecosystem.
