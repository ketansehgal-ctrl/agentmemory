# Off-Page & AI Visibility Checklist

Real backlinks come from places that list, review, or write about you. Every item below uses **your own account** — none of it can be done "without connecting your own account," and no tool can give you other users' backlinks. Skip anyone selling bulk links or link swaps; Google penalizes those.

## Week 1 — Foundations (both businesses)
- [ ] **Google Business Profile** — Quantum Accord Business Group, Darjeeling. Category: "Exporter" (+ "Software company" if the services side is a separate listing). Add photos, phone, WhatsApp, both websites.
- [ ] **Google Search Console** + **Bing Webmaster Tools** — verify both domains, submit sitemaps.
- [ ] **LinkedIn company page** — link both sites; post product/service updates weekly.
- [ ] Make name, address, phone **identical** everywhere (NAP consistency).

## Export side (quantumaccord.shop)
- [ ] B2B marketplaces: **IndiaMART**, **TradeIndia**, **ExportersIndia**, **GlobalLinker**, **Alibaba**, **Go4WorldBusiness**. Link each profile back to the matching product page, not just the homepage.
- [ ] Member directories you already qualify for: **FIEO**, **GCCI**, and the Tea Board of India exporter listing if registered.
- [ ] Ask 3–5 past buyers for a short review/testimonial (Google profile + website).
- [ ] Pitch 2–3 tea blogs or trade publications with a story angle: "a Darjeeling-based exporter on what the 2026 flush season looks like."
- [ ] Attend or list on trade fairs (e.g. India International Tea Convention, Gulfood, SIAL) — exhibitor pages link to your site.

## Services side (quantumaccordbg.com)
- [ ] Agency directories: **Clutch**, **GoodFirms**, **DesignRush** (free profiles).
- [ ] Partner/showcase pages: check if **Vapi** or other tools you use have a partner/agency directory.
- [ ] Publish 1–2 case studies (your own export business's WhatsApp/voice setup counts) and share on LinkedIn.

## AI visibility (getting cited by ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini)
AI assistants mostly cite pages that rank in normal search and that answer questions plainly. So:
- [ ] FAQ sections with direct, factual answers ("What is the MOQ for Darjeeling tea?" → "[your MOQ]").
- [ ] Certification numbers and specific facts in text, not only in images.
- [ ] Consistent listings across the directories above — AI tools cross-check.
- [ ] Blog guides that answer importer questions (see `content/`).

## Once Semrush has API units
Run `backlinks_research` on 3–5 competing Darjeeling exporters and automation agencies to find directories and publications that already link to them — those become your outreach list.
